# Design Principles

## Fun over accuracy

Fun (in play) is more important than historical accuracy or realism.

An example is the campaign map: depending on game settings, it may be circumnavigated on **both**
axes, and it may contain **void tiles** which cannot be traversed or seen around. Obviously, voids
don't exist on a real planet — but they make for more interesting play, so they stay.

This principle is the tie-breaker whenever a realism concern and a playability concern pull in
different directions: choose the more fun option.
